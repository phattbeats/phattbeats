## I never called myself a developer.

Other people did. It took me a while to believe them.

I come from the analog side — hands-on, physical, real. Somewhere along the way I started dragging the analog world into the digital one: a broadcast TV simulator running real cable-channel logic on a Raspberry Pi, a comic library with actual metadata, a house that runs on software two people share. Bridging that gap became the whole thing.

Then this happened:

> **[linuxserver/docker-socket-proxy — PR #51, merged.](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-socket-proxy/pull/51)**
> A real fix, to a real project, that thousands of homelabs depend on. Found in production, diagnosed at 2am energy, submitted upstream instead of patched locally and forgotten.

That one was a personal goal for a long time. It's on the board now.

### What I do

**I run [PHATT TECH](https://github.com/phattbeats)** — a one-human company with an AI agent workforce. I don't write most of the code line-by-line; I architect, direct, verify, and ship. The agents do the typing. I do the seeing. Together we've shipped more working software in a year than I thought I'd touch in a lifetime:

- **[homestead](https://github.com/phattbeats/homestead)** — a shared-life app for two people. Tasks that take turns, lists that sync, a porch you can invite people onto.
- **[phatt-picks](https://github.com/phattbeats/phatt-picks)** — CS2 Major Pick'Em companion, live Valve data and all.
- **[FieldStation42](https://github.com/phattbeats/FieldStation42)** *(fork)* — retro broadcast TV, resurrected.
- **[obsessed](https://github.com/phattbeats/obsessed)** — trivia about *you*, scraped from your own digital footprint.
- **[anthropic-proxy](https://github.com/phattbeats/anthropic-proxy)** — glue code born of pure stubbornness.
